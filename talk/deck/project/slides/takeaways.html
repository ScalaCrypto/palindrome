What the palindrome taught us

The ideas were there in 2.5. Each release made them cheaper to write.

Machinery on demand

Generics, recursion, type classes, ADTs, builders, extensions: each arrived when the problem asked.

Not every step was a win

CanBuildFrom worked, and scared people. 2.13's refinement and wrapper were a detour.

Scala 3 collapses the ceremony

given, enum and extension say what 2.x had to spell out.

The double act closes: the old-ways speaker names what was lost or hard (CanBuildFrom's signatures, the 2.13 wart, value classes that box), the new-ways speaker what Scala 3 fixed. Keep it honest, not nostalgic.
