Two changes. @tailrec turns a promise into a check: the generated code is unchanged. And the collections redesign: SeqLike[A, Repr] names the concrete type, and CanBuildFrom is a factory for builders of it. bf(xs.repr) gives a builder, and we fill it. Now palindromize("abcb") is the String "abcba". Be honest: it worked, and the signatures scared people.
